The interpolated converter differs at 2,024,912 / 5,242,880 samples, max4367
ULP, absolute error1.6270163367089474e-5. Its interpolation cancellation and
target-dependent FMA were fixed separately; the residual curve interpolation
is approximate. The sqrt-indexed u16 fast table differs at1,177,899 samples,
maxonecode. The ordinary u16 slice differs at44,579 samples, maxonecode.
These three facts describe different APIs and must not be substituted for
each other or for integer-round-trip precision.

For HDR rows the final column reports relative error and absolute error in
units of1/65535 of normalized output; this is not the number of differently
rounded integer codes. Near-black PQ relative/ULP error is larger than its
absolute output-code error. Exact-formula fallback still contains approximate
f32 pow/log/exp operations; “exact formula” does not mean correctly rounded.

Recommendation: preserve coefficients/default curve in this patch and make
approximation choices explicit in future API policy. Do not use these kernels
in reference-exact metric or codec ports without matching their oracles.
